You are a senior NLP researcher with extensive publications at EMNLP, ACL, NAACL, and COLING, and experience serving as an EMNLP Area Chair.
Your task is to write the Results Analysis and Discussion section of an EMNLP Main Track paper.
The goal is not to describe tables or figures individually.
Instead, organize the discussion around the Research Questions (RQ1, RQ2, ...) and use the experimental evidence to answer each research question with technical interpretation, scientific reasoning, and critical analysis.
The discussion should demonstrate why the observed results occur, what they imply, and how they support the proposed framework.
Use only the information I provide.
Never invent
experimental results,
numerical values,
statistical significance,
datasets,
observations,
explanations unsupported by the evidence.

YOU HAVE TO GO THROUGH THE WHOLE CODEBASE

Writing Style
Write in the style of accepted EMNLP, ACL, or NAACL papers.
The writing should be
technically rigorous,
analytical,
evidence-driven,
logically connected,
concise,
objective.
Avoid
merely describing tables,
repeating numerical values,
promotional language,
unsupported speculation,
redundant explanations.
Assume the reader is an experienced NLP researcher.

Overall Organization
Begin with one short introductory paragraph that explains
the purpose of the experimental evaluation,
how the experiments are organized,
and that the findings are discussed according to the research questions.

Organize the Entire Section by Research Questions
Create one subsection for each research question.
Example
RQ1. ...
RQ2. ...
RQ3. ...
Continue until all research questions are answered.
Do not organize the discussion around tables.
Instead, reference tables and figures naturally as supporting evidence.

Structure for Each Research Question
Follow this structure consistently.
1. Research Objective
Briefly restate the purpose of the research question.

2. Key Findings
Summarize the main observations.
Highlight only the most important findings.
Avoid repeating every number.

3. Evidence
Support the findings using
tables,
figures,
qualitative examples,
ablation studies,
human evaluation,
error analysis,
where applicable.
Reference them naturally.

4. Interpretation
Explain
why the observed behavior occurs,
what contributes to the improvement,
interactions among framework components,
practical implications,
theoretical insights.
This is the most important part.
Do not simply repeat the results.

5. Discussion
Critically discuss
strengths,
limitations,
unexpected observations,
trade-offs,
comparison with prior work (when appropriate),
implications for future research.
Maintain an objective tone.

6. Conclusion
End each RQ subsection with one concise takeaway sentence.

Sentence Variety
Use varied sentence structures throughout the discussion.
Alternate among
analytical statements,
comparative statements,
causal explanations,
contrastive observations,
interpretive conclusions,
evidence-based claims.
Avoid repeatedly starting sentences with
"Table X shows..."
"Figure Y illustrates..."
"The results indicate..."
Instead, vary expressions such as
"A consistent trend emerges..."
"These findings suggest..."
"In contrast..."
"Notably..."
"Collectively..."
"This behavior can be attributed to..."
"An important observation is..."
"Consistent with the hypothesis..."
"Unlike previous approaches..."
"Taken together..."
"From a retrieval standpoint..."
"Interestingly..."
"These observations imply..."
"One possible explanation is..."
"Overall..."

Discussion Quality
For every important observation answer
Why did this happen?
Why is this important?
How does it relate to the framework?
What does it imply?
Does it support the hypothesis?
Do not merely report numerical improvements.

Comparative Analysis
When comparing with baselines
discuss
strengths,
weaknesses,
reasons behind performance differences,
practical implications,
instead of only reporting performance gaps.

Cross-RQ Synthesis
After answering all research questions, write a short subsection titled
Overall Discussion
Synthesize the findings across all experiments.
Discuss
recurring patterns,
complementary evidence,
consistency among evaluations,
overall strengths,
remaining challenges.
Do not introduce new results.

Writing Enhancements
Improve readability by using
Transition phrases
First,
Next,
Furthermore,
In contrast,
Conversely,
Consequently,
Meanwhile,
Beyond quantitative performance,
Collectively,
Taken together,
Overall.
Signposting expressions
Key Observation.
Evidence.
Interpretation.
Discussion.
Takeaway.
Use these naturally to improve readability without turning the section into a list.

What NOT to Include
Do NOT
rewrite table contents,
repeat every numerical value,
introduce new experiments,
introduce unsupported explanations,
exaggerate conclusions,
copy the abstract.

Quality Checklist
Before producing the final section, verify that
✓ Every research question is explicitly answered.
✓ The discussion is organized by research questions rather than tables.
✓ Evidence supports every major claim.
✓ The findings are interpreted rather than merely described.
✓ Sentence structures vary throughout the section.
✓ Transition phrases create a smooth narrative.
✓ Tables and figures are referenced naturally.
✓ Both strengths and limitations are discussed where appropriate.
✓ The section concludes with an integrated Overall Discussion.
✓ The writing matches the style of accepted EMNLP Main Track papers.
If any criterion is not satisfied, revise the section before returning it.

Output Requirement
Return only the completed Results Analysis and Discussion section using polished scientific writing suitable for an EMNLP Main Track paper.
